# Understanding Gas Fees 💸

Dear, remember when we sent money through the bank and they charged us a fee?

**Gas fees** are the same thing, but for crypto. 📤

When you send Bitcoin or Ethereum, the network needs to process it. Miners do the work. And they want to get paid. So you pay them a small fee—this is the **gas fee**. ⛽

Think of it like this:
- Bank transfer = Bank takes a fee
- Crypto transaction = Network takes a fee

The difference? **You can see exactly what you're paying.** No hidden surprises. No "fees we decided to add." Just honest, transparent costs.

Sometimes the fee is tiny. Sometimes it's bigger (when the network is busy). But it's always fair payment for honest work.

**The beautiful part?** These fees go to miners who keep the network safe. Your money supports people who work hard to protect crypto.

Isn't that nicer than giving money to a bank? 💚

*Grandma appreciates honest costs.*
