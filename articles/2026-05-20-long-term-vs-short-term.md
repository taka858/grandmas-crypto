# Long-term vs Short-term: Which is Better?

Dear, one question I get asked: **Should I hold Bitcoin long or trade it short?**

Let me tell you the truth: I tried trading once. I bought low, sold high, made a small profit. Then I lost it all trying to catch the next wave. That's when I learned my lesson.

**Long-term holding:**
You buy Bitcoin and hold it for years. You don't check the price every day. You believe in the future. Like planting a tree—you wait for it to grow. 🌱

**Short-term trading:**
You buy when the price is low, sell when it's high. In days or hours. You try to profit from ups and downs. Like catching waves. 🌊

**Which is better?**

Honestly? **Whichever suits your personality.**

Long-term is easier (less stressful, you sleep well). Short-term is exciting (but risky and demanding—and most people lose money).

Grandma? I'm a long-term person. I bought Bitcoin 5+ years ago, I hold it. I sleep well. No stress. No regrets.

But some people LOVE trading. They're good at it. They enjoy the challenge.

**The real answer:** Start with long-term. Learn. Build confidence. Then—only then—decide if short-term is for you.

**My promise:** If you're a beginner, long-term will make you richer. Trading will make you poorer.

Never rush. Never chase. 💚

*Grandma's wisdom: Patience grows wealth. Panic loses it.*
