# Distributed Ledgers Explained 📔

Dear, imagine you and your friends keep a **shared diary**. 📖

Everyone has an identical copy. When someone writes a new entry, everyone's diary updates at the same time. If someone tries to erase an old entry? Everyone else's copy still has it. Caught!

This is a **distributed ledger**.

Instead of one person (a bank) keeping the record, thousands of people keep copies. Bitcoin uses this system. Everyone has the same ledger—the same list of all transactions.

**The magic:**
- Nobody can cheat (everyone watches)
- Nobody can delete history (it's everywhere)
- Nobody needs permission (it's decentralized)

A bank has ONE ledger they control. Crypto has THOUSANDS of ledgers, all identical.

Which would you rather trust? One person with a pen? Or thousands of honest people with copies? 💚

*Grandma votes for transparency.*
