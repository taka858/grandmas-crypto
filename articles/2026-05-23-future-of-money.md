# The Future of Money 🌍

Dear, I want to dream with you about the **future of money**.

Today, we need banks. We need governments. We need middlemen to handle our money.

But what if we didn't?

What if money could travel instantly around the world? What if anyone could send it anywhere without asking permission? What if there were no fees, no borders, no gatekeepers? 🚀

**That's the dream of crypto.**

Not to replace your bank tomorrow. But to give you **choice**. 

Maybe you trust your bank—fine! Use it. But maybe you want freedom—crypto is there.

In the future, I believe:
- Money flows freely across borders
- People control their own wealth
- Honest work is rewarded fairly
- Nobody cheats the system

This isn't about getting rich. It's about **freedom**. It's about **dignity**. It's about a world where anyone, anywhere, can be their own banker. 💚

I won't live to see that world fully bloom. But you might. And that makes me hopeful.

*Grandma believes in a kinder future of money.*
