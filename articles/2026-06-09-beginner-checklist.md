# Beginner's Crypto Checklist

Dear, before you start, check these boxes.

**Security:**
☐ I have a strong password (16+ characters, mixed case, numbers, symbols)
☐ I enabled 2FA on my exchange account
☐ I wrote down my seed phrase on PAPER
☐ I stored the paper safely (not digitally)

**Knowledge:**
☐ I understand Bitcoin and Ethereum
☐ I know what a wallet is
☐ I know what HODL means
☐ I know the difference between hot and cold storage

**Planning:**
☐ I have a budget ($50-100 to start)
☐ I know I can afford to lose this money
☐ I plan to hold for at least 5 years
☐ I won't check prices daily

**Preparation:**
☐ I chose an exchange (Coinbase, Kraken, Gemini)
☐ I verified my identity
☐ I understand the tax implications
☐ I know what scams look like

**If all boxes are checked:** You're ready. Start small. Good luck.

**If any box is unchecked:** Read more first. No rush.
