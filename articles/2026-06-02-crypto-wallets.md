# Types of Crypto Wallets

Dear, your crypto needs a home. That home is called a wallet.

Four types:

**1. Exchange Wallets** (easiest)
- Coins live on Coinbase, Kraken, Binance
- You don't control keys
- Risk: If exchange hacks, coins at risk
- Best for: Beginners, active traders

**2. Software Wallets** (easy)
- App on phone/computer
- You control keys
- Risk: Computer hack = coins at risk
- Best for: Intermediate users

**3. Hardware Wallets** (-300)
- Physical device, offline storage
- Safest option
- Even if computer hacked, coins safe
- Best for: Long-term holders

**4. Paper Wallets** (safest but inconvenient)
- Private key written on paper
- Completely offline
- Risk: Paper burn, loss, water damage

My strategy:
- Start with exchange wallet (learn)
- Move 80% to software wallet (,000+)
- Buy hardware wallet (,000+)

Never share private keys. Write down seed phrase on PAPER ONLY.
