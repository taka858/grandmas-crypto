# Crypto Scams to Avoid 🚨

Dear, the crypto world is wonderful. But it has wolves too. Let me teach you to recognize them.

**Scam #1: The Promise**
"Send me $100, I'll turn it into $1,000!" 
❌ FAKE. Nobody can guarantee returns. Run away.

**Scam #2: The Stranger**
Someone on the internet: "I'm a crypto expert. Give me your coins to invest!"
❌ NEVER. Your coins are YOUR responsibility. Not theirs.

**Scam #3: The Phishing Link**
You get an email: "Click here to verify your wallet!"
❌ FAKE. Real companies never ask for passwords via email.

**Scam #4: The FOMO Pressure**
"Bitcoin is up 50% TODAY! You must buy NOW or regret it!"
❌ PANIC. Scammers use fear. Never decide in panic.

**Scam #5: The Too-Good Offer**
"Free crypto! Just register here!"
❌ Nothing is free. There's always a catch.

**Grandma's Safety Rules:**
✅ Never share your secret key
✅ Never click suspicious links
✅ Never trust promises of guaranteed returns
✅ Never send coins to strangers
✅ When in doubt, do nothing

Stay safe, dear. Your money is your freedom. 💚

*Grandma's wisdom: Skepticism is your best defense.*
