# Grandma's 10 Lessons for Crypto Success

Dear, after decades in this space, here are my non-negotiable rules.

**1. Understand before investing**
If you don't understand it, don't buy it. Period.

**2. Start small**
$50-100 is enough to learn. Never risk what you can't afford to lose.

**3. Do your own research**
Don't follow influencers. Don't copy others. Think for yourself.

**4. HODL for years**
Trading daily = losing daily. Years = winning.

**5. Ignore the noise**
Bitcoin crashed 80%+ five times. It recovered every time. Ignore the panic.

**6. Diversify always**
All Bitcoin = risky. Mix Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins, alternatives.

**7. Never chase FOMO**
Buying at the peak is how you lose. Wait for the dip.

**8. Secure your keys**
Private key = your crypto. Lose it = lost forever. Paper only.

**9. Pay your taxes**
Report honestly. Crypto is money. Money gets taxed.

**10. Sleep well**
If you're checking prices hourly, you invested too much. Peace of mind > profit.

**The truth:** Crypto is not gambling if you're patient. It's patience that builds wealth.

Not luck. Not timing. Patience.
