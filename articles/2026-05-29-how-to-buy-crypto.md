Dear, you want to buy crypto but don't know where to start?

I'm going to make it simple. Step by step.

**Step 1: Choose an Exchange**

An exchange is like a bank for crypto. You pick one, open an account, and buy coins there.

**Popular exchanges:**
- Coinbase (easiest for beginners)
- Kraken (very reliable)
- Gemini (user-friendly)
- Binance (most popular worldwide)

Which one? Pick **Coinbase** or **Kraken** for your first time. They're simple.

**Step 2: Create Your Account**

1. Go to the website (e.g., coinbase.com)
2. Click "Sign up"
3. Enter your email
4. Create a password (STRONG! Numbers + letters + symbols)
5. Verify your email
6. Add your phone number (for security)

That's it. You have an account.

**Step 3: Verify Your Identity**

The exchange needs to know you're real (government rules).

You'll upload:
- A photo of your ID (passport, driver's license)
- A selfie of yourself

Takes 5 minutes. This is normal and safe.

**Step 4: Add Money**

Now you have money to buy crypto.

Your options:
- Bank transfer (cheapest, but slower - 3-5 days)
- Debit card (faster, but costs a small fee)
- Credit card (instant, but EXPENSIVE fees)

**My tip:** Use bank transfer. Save money.

**Step 5: Buy Your First Coin**

1. Go to "Buy" section
2. Choose Bitcoin or Ethereum (safest for beginners)
3. Enter how much money you want to spend
4. Click "Buy"
5. Done! You own crypto!

**Step 6: Move to Your Wallet (Optional)**

Your coins are safe on the exchange. BUT if you want extra safety, move them to your own wallet.

A wallet is like your personal safe. Only YOU have the key.

Learn about wallets later. For now, leave your coins on the exchange.

**How much should you buy?**

Start small. **$50-100 is perfect.**

Why? So if something goes wrong, you don't lose much while learning.
