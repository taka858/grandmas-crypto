# How to Buy Your First Crypto

Dear, buying crypto is simple.

Step 1: Choose an exchange (Coinbase, Kraken, Gemini).
Step 2: Create an account.
Step 3: Verify your identity.
Step 4: Add money (bank transfer is cheapest).
Step 5: Buy Bitcoin or Ethereum.
Step 6: Done!

How much? Start small. -100 is perfect.

Remember: Prices change fast. You could lose money. Only invest what you can afford to lose.

But here's good news: The process is simple. Money is protected. You can sell anytime.

Don't wait for perfect timing. The best time was years ago. The second best time is TODAY.
