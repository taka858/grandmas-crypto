# How to Buy Your First Crypto

Dear, buying crypto is simple. And you can do this today.

My first purchase was scary. I nervously clicked "buy" for the first time, thinking I was about to lose everything. But you know what? I didn't. And 5 years later, I'm glad I did it.

**Here's how simple it is:**

Step 1: Choose an exchange (Coinbase, Kraken, Gemini).
Step 2: Create an account.
Step 3: Verify your identity.
Step 4: Add money (bank transfer is cheapest).
Step 5: Buy Bitcoin or Ethereum.
Step 6: Done!

**How much should you start with?**

$50-100 is perfect. Not enough to hurt if you lose it. Enough to learn and feel the excitement.

**But be honest with yourself:**

Prices change fast. You could lose money. Only invest what you can afford to lose—and truly afford to lose without it affecting your life.

**Here's the good news:** The process is simple. Your money is protected. You can sell anytime. Most exchanges have insurance. It's safer than you think.

**My final thought:**

Don't wait for the "perfect" price. I waited 5 years after first hearing about Bitcoin because I was afraid. The best time to plant a tree was 20 years ago. The second best time is today.

Your turn. 💚
