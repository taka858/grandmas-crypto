# How to Build Your Crypto Portfolio

Dear, putting all eggs in one basket is risky. Build a PORTFOLIO.

**The 3-part portfolio:**

**1. Core Holdings (50-60%)**
- Bitcoin, Ethereum
- Proven, safest
- Boring but reliable

**2. Alternative Coins (30-40%)**
- Solana, Polkadot, Chainlink
- Growth potential
- More risky

**3. Stablecoins (10-20%)**
- USDC, USDT, DAI
- Peace of mind
- Buy dips, earn interest

**Example portfolios:**

Conservative: 70% Bitcoin, 20% Ethereum, 10% Stablecoins

Balanced: 50% Bitcoin, 20% Ethereum, 15% Alt coins, 15% Stablecoins

Aggressive: 30% Bitcoin, 20% Ethereum, 40% Alt coins, 10% Stablecoins

**Build slowly:**
- Week 1: Buy 
- Week 2: Buy 
- Week 3: Buy 
- Week 4: Buy 

Dollar Cost Averaging reduces risk.

**Rebalance yearly.**

Once per year, check percentages. If Bitcoin is 60% instead of 50%, sell some. Buy others.
