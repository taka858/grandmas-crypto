# How to Build Your Crypto Portfolio 📊

Dear, putting all your eggs in one basket is risky.

That's why we BUILD A PORTFOLIO.

A portfolio is a mix of different cryptos. Some safe. Some risky. Together, they balance each other.

Let me teach you how.

**Why Portfolio Matters**

❌ All Bitcoin = if Bitcoin crashes, you lose everything  
✅ Bitcoin + Ethereum + Stablecoin = more stable  

Diversification = sleeping peacefully.

---

## **THE 3-PART PORTFOLIO**

### **1. Core Holdings (50-60%)** 🏛️

**What:** Big, established coins.

**Examples:**
- Bitcoin (the safest)
- Ethereum (second safest)

**Why:** These have survived crashes. They're proven.

**Risk Level:** Low

**Why hold:** They're the foundation. Boring but reliable.

---

### **2. Alternative Coins (30-40%)** 🚀

**What:** Newer, interesting projects.

**Examples:**
- Solana
- Polkadot
- Chainlink
- Your favorite Layer 2

**Why:** Growth potential. More exciting than Bitcoin.

**Risk Level:** Medium

**Why hold:** Some might explode in value. Some might go to zero.

---

### **3. Stablecoins (10-20%)** 💵

**What:** Coins tied to USD. Don't change price much.

**Examples:**
- USDC
- USDT
- DAI

**Why:** Peace of mind. Dry powder for opportunities.

**Risk Level:** Very Low

**Why hold:** 
- When market crashes, buy the dip
- Earn interest (yield farming)
- Sleep at night

---

## **PORTFOLIO EXAMPLES**

### **CONSERVATIVE (Risk-Averse)**
- 70% Bitcoin
- 20% Ethereum
- 10% USDC

**Good for:** People who want to sleep.

---

### **BALANCED (Goldilocks)**
- 50% Bitcoin
- 20% Ethereum
- 15% Alt coins (Solana, Polkadot)
- 15% Stablecoins

**Good for:** Most people. Safe but growth.

---

### **AGGRESSIVE (High Risk)**
- 30% Bitcoin
- 20% Ethereum
- 40% Alt coins (DeFi, Layer 2, emerging projects)
- 10% Stablecoins

**Good for:** Young investors. Can afford losses.

---

## **HOW TO BUILD YOUR PORTFOLIO**

### **Step 1: Decide Your Risk Level**

Are you:
- ⚪ Conservative? (want safety)
- 🟡 Balanced? (want growth + safety)
- 🔴 Aggressive? (want growth, can lose money)

Pick one. Honest answer. 💡

### **Step 2: Choose Your Mix**

Using the examples above, pick percentages.

Example: "I want 50/20/20/10"
- 50% Bitcoin
- 20% Ethereum
- 20% Alt coins
- 10% Stablecoins

### **Step 3: Calculate Amounts**

If you have $1,000:
- Bitcoin: $500
- Ethereum: $200
- Alt coins: $200
- Stablecoins: $100

### **Step 4: Buy Over Time**

Don't buy all at once!

Use Dollar Cost Averaging:
- Week 1: Buy $250 worth
- Week 2: Buy $250 worth
- Week 3: Buy $250 worth
- Week 4: Buy $250 worth

Why? Reduces risk if price crashes next day.

### **Step 5: Hold & Rebalance**

Don't panic sell. Hold for at least 1 year.

Once per year:
- Check your percentages
- If Bitcoin is now 60% instead of 50%, sell some
- Buy other coins to get back to 50%

This is called "rebalancing."

❌ FOMO buying at peak prices

❌ Believing someone will 100x quick

✅ Rebalance yearly  
✅ Be patient

## **REBALANCING EXAMPLE**

You start with $1,000:
- Bitcoin: $500 (50%)
- Ethereum: $300 (30%)
- Alt: $200 (20%)

After 1 year, Bitcoin booms to $2,000 and now your portfolio is:
- Bitcoin: $1,000 (66%)
- Ethereum: $300 (20%)
- Alt: $200 (14%)

Time to rebalance! Sell $333 of Bitcoin. Buy Ethereum and Alt coins.

Now you're back to:
- Bitcoin: $667 (50%)
- Ethereum: $500 (30%)
- Alt: $500 (20%)

This locks in profits from Bitcoin and buys the dips.
