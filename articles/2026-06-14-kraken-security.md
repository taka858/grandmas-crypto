# Staying Safe on Kraken

Dear, you have an account and crypto. Keep it safe.

**Enable 2FA**
Google Authenticator or Authy. Non-negotiable.

**Don't Share Your Password**
Not with friends. Not with "support." NOBODY.

**Don't Save Passwords**
Use a password manager (1Password, LastPass).

**Check Your Email**
Kraken sends you login alerts. If you didn't log in, change password immediately.

**Don't Keep Large Amounts**
,000+ ? Move to a wallet.
-500 ? Keep on Kraken. It's safe.

**Never Click Links in Emails**
Even if it says "Kraken Support," type the website yourself.

**That's It.**
Follow these 6 rules. You're secure.

Security is boring. That's the point.
