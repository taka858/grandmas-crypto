# Setting Up Kraken (5 Minutes)

Dear, opening a Kraken account is simple. Here's how.

**Step 1: Go to Kraken.com**
Visit the website. Click "Sign Up."

**Step 2: Create Account**
Email + password. Make it strong (16+ characters).

**Step 3: Verify Email**
Check your inbox. Click the link.

**Step 4: Enable 2FA**
Use Google Authenticator or Authy. IMPORTANT.

**Step 5: Verify Identity**
Upload ID. Take a selfie. 5 minutes tops.

**Step 6: Add Payment Method**
Bank transfer (cheapest) or debit card (faster).

**Done!**
You can now buy crypto.

**Total time: 10 minutes.**

No pressure. No hurry. You're just setting up.
