# What is Web3?

Dear, the internet is changing. Let me explain.

**Web1 (1990s-2000s):**
Websites. You read. That's it. Companies controlled everything.

**Web2 (2000s-now):**
Social media. You create content. But Big Tech owns it, controls it, profits from it. They own YOUR data.

**Web3 (now and future):**
You own your data. You own your content. You own your money. Blockchain makes this possible.

**How?**

In Web2:
- You post a photo on Facebook
- Facebook owns the photo
- Facebook makes money from ads
- You get nothing

In Web3:
- You post a photo on a blockchain
- YOU own the photo (with a private key)
- You control who sees it
- You decide if you want to sell it
- YOU make the money

**Real examples of Web3:**

1. **NFTs** - You own digital art. Nobody can copy it or take it.

2. **DeFi** - You control your money. No bank, no Big Tech.

3. **DAOs** - Community groups where everyone votes. No CEO, no boss.

4. **Decentralized social media** - Twitter without Elon. You own your followers.

**The promise:**

No middleman. No billionaire tech CEO. No taking your data. No shadow banning.

Just you, your data, your money, your power.

**The reality:**

Web3 is still early. Some projects are real. Some are scams.

It's like the internet in 1995. Most will fail. A few will change the world.

**My take:**

Web3 is not about getting rich quick.

It's about taking back control of YOUR digital life.

That's why I believe in it.
