# What's Next After Day 1?

Dear, you bought your first crypto. Now what?

**Month 1: Learn**
- Follow the price (but don't panic)
- Read about DeFi
- Join a crypto community (Discord, Reddit)
- Keep learning

**Month 2-3: Diversify**
- Buy some Ethereum
- Add stablecoins to your portfolio
- Research alternative coins
- Don't rush

**Month 4-6: Earn**
- Try staking (earn interest on your coins)
- Research DeFi lending
- Keep holding
- Ignore the noise

**Year 1: Patience**
- Don't sell in panic
- Don't buy in FOMO
- Keep holding
- Rebalance once

**Year 5+: Wealth**
- You're now a long-term holder
- You understand crypto deeply
- Your patience is paying off
- Keep going

**The secret:** 
Every day you hold is a day closer to wealth.

Not luck. Not timing. Time.
