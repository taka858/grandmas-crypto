# Understanding DeFi: What It Can Do For You

Dear, DeFi lets your money WORK for you.

In banks: ,000 earns .10/year (0.01%).
In DeFi: ,000 earns -100/year (4-10%).

**Four methods:**

**1. Staking** (easiest)
- Hold coin, network pays you
- Ethereum: 4% per year
- Risk: Low. Coin value could drop.

**2. Lending**
- Lend coins to borrowers
- They pay 5-10% interest
- Risk: Medium. Borrower defaults possible.

**3. Liquidity Pools**
- Provide two coins for trading
- Earn fees
- Risk: Medium-High. Prices move = lose money.

**4. Yield Farming**
- Deposit coins in special contracts
- Earn 10-50% per year
- Risk: High. Smart contracts can break.

**My strategy:**
- 50% just holding
- 30% staking
- 15% lending
- 5% cash (emergency)

**Important:**
Start small. Don't expect magic. DeFi is still new. Bugs happen. Use trusted platforms (Aave, Uniswap).

If you understand DeFi, you understand crypto. That's valuable.
