# What are NFTs? 🎨

Dear, let me explain something that sounds complicated but is actually beautiful—**NFTs**.

NFT stands for "Non-Fungible Token." That's a fancy way of saying **unique digital thing that belongs to YOU**. 

Think of it like this:

I have a painting. It's one-of-a-kind. Nobody else has it. I can sell it, and the new owner has proof it's real. 🖼️

**NFTs are the same**, but digital.

You create digital art. You mint it as an NFT. Now it has a certificate of authenticity on the blockchain. Anyone who buys it gets proof they own the REAL one (not a copy).

**Why does this matter?**

For the first time in history, digital things can be **truly yours**. 

Before, if you bought digital art, it was just a file. Anyone could copy it. But with NFTs? You own THE original. Proof is on the blockchain forever.

**The dream?**
Artists get paid fairly. Collectors own truly rare things. No middleman.

Is it perfect? No. But it's revolutionary. 💚

*Grandma thinks the future of art is on the blockchain.*
