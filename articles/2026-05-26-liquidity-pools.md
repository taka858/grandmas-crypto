# Liquidity Pools Explained

Dear, imagine farmers putting goods on a stand and customers trading freely. That's a liquidity pool.

You provide two coins. Traders swap them. You earn fees from each trade.

It's passive income—money without effort.

But if prices change a lot, you might lose money. Only for people comfortable with risk.

Start small. Learn as you go.
