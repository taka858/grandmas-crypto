**Liquidity pools are like that.**

In DeFi, people need to trade coins. But normally, you need a buyer AND a seller at the same time. What if they're not both there?

**Enter liquidity pools.**

You put TWO types of coins into a pool (like Bitcoin + Ethereum). Other people come and trade. When they do:
- The pool automatically gives them the coin they want
- You get a reward for providing the coins! ðŸ’°

**Think of it like this:**
- Old way: Bank holds money, makes profit
- Pool way: YOU hold money in a pool, YOU make the profit

**How much can you earn?**
- Depends on how busy the pool is
- Busier = more trades = bigger rewards for you

**The risk?**
If coin prices change a lot, you could lose money. So only put in coins you can afford to hold long-term.
