# Understanding Blockchain 📖

Hello dear, today I want to explain something magical called **blockchain**.

Think of it like our **town's message board**. 📌

When I write a message, everyone in town gets a copy. If someone tries to change my message, everyone notices because their copies don't match. So nobody can cheat!

Blockchain works the same way. Every transaction gets written down, and thousands of computers keep copies. If someone tries to lie about money, all the other computers say "No, that's wrong!"

It's like the whole town watching your wallet. Safe, honest, and transparent. ✨

That's the magic of blockchain—the **power of many eyes watching together**. 👀💚

*Grandma trusts what can be checked.*
