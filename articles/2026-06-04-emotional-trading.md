# Staying Calm in the Crypto World

Dear, crypto prices go UP. Crypto prices go DOWN. That's normal.

But your EMOTIONS? That's where people get hurt.

When Bitcoin jumps 20%, everyone celebrates. Then you feel scared. FOMO kicks in.

You buy at the TOP. Price crashes. You panic SELL. You miss recovery.

This cycle repeats. Most people lose money.

**Simple rules I follow:**

1. Make a plan BEFORE buying. Write it down. STICK TO IT.

2. Don't check prices daily. Seriously.
   - Checking every hour = crazy emotions
   - Checking monthly = you stay calm

3. Have boring conviction. Bitcoin crashed 80%+ five times. It recovered every time.

4. Emotions are normal. Feel scared? Feel excited? That's OK. Just don't let emotions decide.

**The secret:**
People who make money hold for YEARS. Not days. Years.

The ones checking prices daily? They usually lose.

I didn't get rich being emotional. I got peaceful being calm.

Peace of mind? Worth more than profit.
