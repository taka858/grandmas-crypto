# Your First Buy on Kraken

Dear, you have an account. Now what?

**Step 1: Add Money**
Bank transfer (takes 3-5 days, free).
Or debit card (instant, small fee).

**Step 2: Go to "Buy"**
Click the "Buy" button. Choose Bitcoin or Ethereum.

**Step 3: Choose Amount**
Start small. -100 is perfect for learning.

**Step 4: Review**
Check the fee. Check the total. Confirm.

**Step 5: Done**
Your first crypto is yours. Congratulations.

**What now?**
- Transfer to a wallet (later, not now)
- Hold for 5+ years
- Don't panic if price drops
- Don't celebrate if price rises

**That's it.**
You're now a crypto owner.
