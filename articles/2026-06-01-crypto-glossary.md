# Crypto Glossary: Essential Terms for Beginners 📚

Dear, crypto has its own language.

Words like "HODL," "whale," and "FUD" might confuse you. Let me explain the most important ones.

Think of this as your crypto dictionary.

---

## 💰 **MONEY & TRADING**

**Crypto / Cryptocurrency**
Digital money you can send online. Bitcoin, Ethereum, etc.

**Bitcoin**
The first and most famous cryptocurrency (2009).

**Ethereum**
A cryptocurrency that also runs smart contracts (programs).

**Altcoin**
Any crypto that's NOT Bitcoin. "Alt" = alternative.

**Stablecoin**
A crypto tied to a real currency (USD, EUR). Value doesn't change much. Example: USDC, USDT.

**Token**
A digital asset on a blockchain. Slightly different from "coin."

**Exchange**
A website where you buy/sell crypto. Coinbase, Kraken, Binance.

**Wallet**
Digital storage for your crypto (like a digital wallet for money).

**Private Key**
Secret code to access your wallet. NEVER share it.

**Seed Phrase**
12-24 magic words that unlock your wallet. Write it down on paper!

---

## 📈 **MARKET TERMS**

**Bull Market**
Prices going UP. Everyone is happy. 🐂

**Bear Market**
Prices going DOWN. Everyone is sad. 🐻

**HODL**
Hold = don't sell. "HODL" became crypto slang for "hold no matter what."

**Moon**
Price going way up. "Bitcoin is going to the moon!" = prices rising.

**Pump and Dump**
Buy low → advertise heavily → price goes up → sell at top → price crashes. (SCAM)

**FOMO**
Fear Of Missing Out. Buying in a panic because price is rising.

**FUD**
Fear, Uncertainty, Doubt. Negative news that scares people.

**Whale**
Someone with LOTS of crypto (100+). They can move markets.

**ATH (All-Time High)**
Highest price ever. "Bitcoin hit a new ATH!"

**ATL (All-Time Low)**
Lowest price ever.

**Market Cap**
Total value of all coins. Market Cap = Price × Total Coins.

**Volume**
Amount of crypto traded today. High volume = active market.

---

## 🔧 **TECHNOLOGY**

**Blockchain**
Database of transactions, shared across many computers. Very secure.

**Block**
A package of transactions, added to the blockchain.

**Hash**
A unique code for each block. Changes one digit = whole hash changes.

**Mining**
Computers solving puzzles to add blocks. Miners get rewards.

**Node**
A computer running blockchain software. Keeps the network alive.

**Smart Contract**
A program that runs automatically when conditions are met.

**Gas Fee**
Cost to do something on blockchain. Like paying for electricity.

**Layer 2**
A faster, cheaper way to use blockchain. Built on top of Layer 1.

**DeFi (Decentralized Finance)**
Financial services without banks. Lending, trading, borrowing.

**NFT (Non-Fungible Token)**
Digital art/collectible, unique and owned by someone.

**Staking**
Holding crypto to support the network. You earn rewards.

**Yield Farming**
Put crypto in a pool, earn interest. Higher risk, higher reward.

---

## 📊 **PRICE ACTIONS**

**Pump**
Price jumps up suddenly.

**Dump**
Price crashes suddenly.

**Correction**
Price goes down after going up (normal).

**Resistance**
Price level where buyers stop. Prices bounce down.

**Support**
Price level where sellers stop. Prices bounce up.

**Breakout**
Price breaks through resistance. Could mean big move coming.

**Dip**
Small price drop. Good time to buy? Maybe.

**Rally**
Price rises over time.

**Consolidation**
Price stays around same level. Before big move.

---

## 👥 **COMMUNITY SLANG**

**Lambo**
"When lambo?" = When will I be rich enough to buy a Lamborghini? (Joke)

**Diamond Hands**
Someone who holds even when prices drop. Strong, doesn't sell. 💎

**Paper Hands**
Someone who sells quickly when scared. Weak, panics easily. 📄

**Bag**
Amount of crypto you own. "I have a big bag of Bitcoin."

**Rug Pull**
Founder disappears with everyone's money. SCAM.

**Exit Scam**
Project stops working, money vanishes. SCAM.

**Shill**
Someone who promotes a coin for money. Paid advertisement.

**Bagholder**
Someone who bought high and holds coins that dropped. Now stuck.

---

## 💡 **INVESTMENT TERMS**

**DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging)**
Buy same amount every week/month. Reduces risk.

**Long**
Bet that price will go UP.

**Short**
Bet that price will go DOWN.

**Leverage**
Borrow money to trade. Risky!

**Liquidation**
Forced sale when you lose too much money.

**Profit Taking**
Sell some coins to lock in gains.

**Rebalancing**
Adjust your portfolio back to target amounts.

---

## ⚠️ **RISK TERMS**

**Volatility**
Price changes a lot. High = risky.

**Risk Management**
Protecting money by diversifying and setting limits.

**Slippage**
Actual price differs from expected. Happens on big trades.

**Impermanent Loss**
Loss from providing liquidity when prices move a lot.

**Counterparty Risk**
Risk that the other party disappears or fails.
