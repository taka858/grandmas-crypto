# Crypto Glossary

Dear, crypto has its own language.

Essential words:

**Money:**
Crypto, Bitcoin, Ethereum, Altcoin, Stablecoin, Token, Exchange, Wallet, Private Key, Seed Phrase

**Market:**
Bull Market (up), Bear Market (down), HODL (hold), Moon (rising), Pump and Dump (scam), FOMO, FUD, Whale, ATH, ATL, Market Cap, Volume

**Technology:**
Blockchain, Block, Hash, Mining, Node, Smart Contract, Gas Fee, Layer 2, DeFi, NFT, Staking, Yield Farming

**Price:**
Pump, Dump, Correction, Resistance, Support, Breakout, Dip, Rally, Consolidation

**Community:**
Lambo, Diamond Hands (strong), Paper Hands (weak), Bag, Rug Pull, Shill, Bagholder

**Investment:**
DCA, Long, Short, Leverage, Liquidation, Profit Taking, Rebalancing

**Risk:**
Volatility, Risk Management, Slippage, Impermanent Loss, Counterparty Risk

Don't know a word? Google it + "crypto." Join communities. Ask questions. Everyone started as a beginner.
