# DeFi Explained

Dear, DeFi means "money without banks." In banks, they control your money. In DeFi, you control it.

When I first heard about DeFi, I thought it was too dangerous. "No banks? That's crazy!" I said. But I've come to understand it, and I'm honestly amazed.

You can lend coins and earn interest. You can borrow without permission. You can trade instantly.

Nobody is in charge. Everyone follows the rules. That's the magic.

I started with tiny amounts in DeFi—just to learn. And today? Some of my coins are earning interest passively. It's remarkable.

Is it perfect? No. It's new and risky. But is it the future of money? I absolutely believe so.

And you can learn it too—safely, slowly, with small amounts first. That's how I did it. 💚
